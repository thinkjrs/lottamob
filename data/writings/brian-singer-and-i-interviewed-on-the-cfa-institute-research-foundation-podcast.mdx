[This episode](https://youtu.be/1ZbzkIaMl1I?si=9tmMYBwS6WsCJ611) of the CFA Institute Research Foundation podcast features our first in-studio recording, for which we went up to Chicago. Anthony DeChellis interviewed us.

Brian Singer and I discuss an important piece of the investment framework that we have spent the last years developing: How much passive versus active allocation to include in a portfolio. Spoiler: it all boils down to your horizon and preferred level of entrepreneurship. The three of us whom you see on screen  are in the process of creating [the organization](<[https://wealthhorizons.com/](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://wealthhorizons.com/&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1756073991059816&usg=AOvVaw1toRGhF3D0M3tffMk4pZnS)>) needed to bring this framework to the world.

Also check out [the piece we published](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/docs/research-reports/moberg_rf_brief_2025_online.pdf?weekend-reading-link_img-17125/) for the Research Foundation on this topic.
