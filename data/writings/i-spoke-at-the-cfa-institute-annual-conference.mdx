This year, the [CFA Institute Annual conference](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/events/2025/may-2025-cfa-institute-live) was finally back after covid! I had the great pleasure to moderate a panel for the CFA Institute Research Foundation on Private Credit for the session hosted by [the CFA Institute Research Foundation](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research-foundation).

I also recorded a host of episodes for [Financial Thought Exchange](<[https://www.youtube.com/@CFA_Research_Foundation](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.youtube.com/@CFA_Research_Foundation&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1756073991066061&usg=AOvVaw3nvVzPAqhiKdB-JPEAoOBX)>), the official podcast of the CFA Institute Research Foundation, as well as one for the [CFA Institute’s YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@cfainstitute). I will post them here as they become available.
