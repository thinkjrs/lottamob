In this [episode](https://youtu.be/SC3xMXzhXPA?si=gHDYl0WR6ud3POKj) of the Financial Thought Podcast, I sit down with Bill Hardin, Dean of the FIU Business School, to discuss the measurable and intangible returns of business education.

We compare the value of technical skills like DCF modeling and the CAPM versus personal networks, and also cover how networking goals differ by career stage. We also touch on why certain personalities benefit disproportionately from the MBA structure, and how pre-existing wealth alters incentives for success.
