This conversation was an absolute blast, as Bob Murphy jumped though my adventures in the Swedish military, my scholarship, \[my book]\( [https://www.amazon.com/Political-Economy-Special-Economic-Zones/dp/1138237817](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.amazon.com/Political-Economy-Special-Economic-Zones/dp/1138237817\&sa=D\&source=editors\&ust=1756073991054619\&usg=AOvVaw3CpKeXTFSxFn6bQzKKmGw-)) and a whole host of other adventures. As fellow economists, Mr. Murphy and I speak the same language, and I had the pleasure to get to know this excellent scholar briefly while living in Chicago. He has \[his own podcast]\(https\://www\.bobmurphyshow\.com/), is a \[a Senior Fellow with the Mises Institute]\([https://mises.org/profile/robert-p-murphy](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://mises.org/profile/robert-p-murphy\&sa=D\&source=editors\&ust=1756073991054982\&usg=AOvVaw020pRHgOpfmicL15gnqMNs)), author of \[numerous books]\( [https://www.amazon.com/stores/author/B001JOYQXI/allbooks?ingress=0\&visitId=6ff7d0a5-6202-47f2-bdf2-5e5a9aa442f4\&ref\_=ap\_rdr\&ccs\_id=1676fe0b-37ec-4070-bcbf-13781f9b07df](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.amazon.com/stores/author/B001JOYQXI/allbooks?ingress%3D0%26visitId%3D6ff7d0a5-6202-47f2-bdf2-5e5a9aa442f4%26ref_%3Dap_rdr%26ccs_id%3D1676fe0b-37ec-4070-bcbf-13781f9b07df\&sa=D\&source=editors\&ust=1756073991055253\&usg=AOvVaw3yaO9-VsGtLp2za2RoPf2E)) and much more.

His company (infineo]\([https://www.infineo.ai/](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.infineo.ai/\&sa=D\&source=editors\&ust=1756073991055388\&usg=AOvVaw1vjZiOxGUc-s9vcKOoilGW)) is digitizing the life insurance industry using blockchain technology to provide friction-free access to one of the most stable assets in the world for both institutional and retail investors at scale.
